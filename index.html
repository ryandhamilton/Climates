What we do

Three lines of work

01

Climate strategic communications

Minimize risk, create value. Positioning, narrative, and messaging that protect your reputation in a contested environment and turn a credible climate position into a commercial and stakeholder advantage.

Grounded in 15 years across government communications, EDF, and global advisory work.

02

Carbon markets commercial advisory

Commercial guidance for credit developers and buyers in carbon and CDR markets. Current work includes advising a livestock supply-chain credit developer: sharpening the buyer-facing pitch, mapping the FLAG-committed buyers for whom insetting is structurally required, and facilitating signed transactions.

The job is converting market complexity into closed deals.

03

Boutique decarbonization strategy

Focused support for organizations building or resetting their decarbonization approach. Senior and hands-on, built for clients who want judgment and execution rather than a large generic engagement.

The newsletter

Because the planet could use a mate.

Climates started as a newsletter, and the newsletter is still the heart of it. Most weeks I take one climate story and strip out the jargon, the shame, and the bullshit. Systems over guilt. Receipts over vibes.

It's written for people who care about the planet and are tired of being lectured about it.
